## About me

I’m **Laksopan Ravindran**, an **Associate Software Engineer at DGateway In** and a freelance full-stack and AI engineer based in Sri Lanka. I build web, mobile, and desktop products that combine reliable software architecture with practical AI capabilities.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- Full-stack product engineering with React, Next.js, Node.js, Django, Laravel, and Spring Boot
- Mobile and cross-platform development with Flutter and Tauri
- Applied AI across NLP, computer vision, generative AI, and on-device inference
- Cloud deployment, secure authentication, REST APIs, testing, and CI/CD

I completed a **B.Sc. (Hons) in Information Technology at SLIIT (2022–2026)** and have professional experience across product development, deployment, optimization, and technical handover.

**Open to:** full-stack engineering, AI-enabled product development, and challenging software projects.
